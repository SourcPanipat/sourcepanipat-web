# SourcePanipat Web (Main Buyer Marketplace)
- **Domain:** `sourcepanipat.com`
- **Target Users:** Pan-India B2B thrift store owners, retailers, and boutique buyers.
- **Key Features:**
  - OLX-style product card listings with video grade previews (Grade A/B/C).
  - Dual purchasing mode: Sealed Bulk Bales (80kg/100kg) vs Hand-Picked Curated Lots (MOQ 25 pcs).
  - Masked supplier display (`#PNP-SELLER-001`).
  - Automated SSR dynamic product indexing (`/bales/[slug]`) with JSON-LD schema for SEO.
  - Escrow checkout with optional ₹1,000 Verified Inspection Shield.
